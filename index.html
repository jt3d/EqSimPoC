The course

Free practice over seven fences. Set your controls and assists, then ride.

Controls

Hold the phone the way you want to play, then tap Set neutral. Tilt right and the dot should move right; tilt the top away from you and it should move up.

Thumb pad: the left stick is your body (side to side, forward and back). Tap anywhere on the right to give an aid. Arrow keys and Space also work on a computer.

Aids and feedback

How to ride
  • Balance: keep the white dot inside the green zone on the balance gauge. Lean into turns. Drift out and you wobble, lose a stirrup, hang off, then fall.
  • Take-off: tap during the stride you want him to leave the ground from. He takes off at the end of that stride. The yellow strip on the ground is the ideal take-off zone; stride guides show where each stride will land.
  • Jump position: lean forward as he takes off (fold), stay forward over the fence, then sit back on landing. Get left behind and you catch him in the mouth.
  • Pace: with Seat lean, lean forward to lengthen the stride and back to shorten it between fences. With Buttons, hold Steady or Push.
  • Leads: away from fences, a tap asks for a flying change. Time it in the moment of suspension: the gap after the third hoofbeat, shown in green on the beat gauge. In the air, lean towards your next turn to land on the right lead.
  • Rules: 4 faults for a pole or a refusal, 1 fault per second (or part) over the time allowed. A second refusal or a fall is elimination.
